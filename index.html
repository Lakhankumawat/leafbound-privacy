Leafbound Privacy Policy

Developer: Bytes Alchemist

Privacy contact: bytes.alchemist@gmail.com

Effective date / last updated: 26 October 2026

Applies to: Leafbound for Android, versions 0.5.11, 0.5.13, 0.5.14 and 0.5.15 (package com.bytesalchemist.leafbound). Where these versions differ, the differences are described below.

Overview

Leafbound is an offline garden game. You do not need an account or sign-in. The current version has no advertising, in-app purchases, online multiplayer or cloud save. The game does not transmit your game progress, preferences or sound recordings to Bytes Alchemist or other parties. It does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

Information used on your device

Leafbound stores the following in app-specific files on your device to provide and remember your game experience:

  • Progress: coins, levels, upgrades, inventory, unlocked gardens and companions, discoveries, active play time and gameplay counters used for progression.
  • Preferences: control settings, sound volumes and mute settings. Version 0.5.11 also saves a frame-rate choice. Versions 0.5.13, 0.5.14 and 0.5.15 run at a fixed 60 FPS target and no longer offer or save that setting; a value saved by version 0.5.11 stays unused on your device until you clear the app's data.
  • Optional recordings: the custom sounds you choose to record for use in the game. These may contain your voice or other sounds around you.

These files are not uploaded by the game. Leafbound does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, photos or shared media library.

Optional microphone recording

The game requests microphone permission only after you tap RECORD on the sounds page (MENU > SOUNDS & MIC in version 0.5.11, MENU > SOUNDS in versions 0.5.13, 0.5.14 and 0.5.15). Granting permission does not start a recording; on Android, you tap RECORD again when ready. You can decline permission and keep playing with the built-in sounds. Permission can also be revoked in Android's app settings.

A visible recording screen shows when the microphone is active. A take lasts up to five seconds. A valid take is saved when you choose STOP & SAVE, or automatically when the timer ends. Saving replaces the previous sound in that category; the game keeps no recording history. Cancel, Back or leaving the app cancels an unfinished take and keeps the previously saved sound. The game does not record in the background.

Audio is processed locally to display its loudness, limit playback volume and save a sound file. It is not transcribed, used to identify you, uploaded or shared by the game. Avoid recording private conversations or another person without their permission.

Storage, retention and deletion

Progress and preferences remain on your device until you reset them or remove the app's data. A saved custom sound remains until you replace it, reset that sound or remove the app's data.

  • RESET on a sound row deletes only that one custom recording and restores the built-in sound. Other recordings, progress and preferences are unchanged.
  • RESET SENSITIVITY on MENU > CONTROLS (versions 0.5.13, 0.5.14 and 0.5.15) restores only the LOOK X and LOOK Y control values. It does not change progress or recordings.
  • RESET PROGRESS (version 0.5.11 only) resets your campaign and progression counters. It does not delete custom recordings or reset your separate sound, control or frame-rate preferences. Versions 0.5.13, 0.5.14 and 0.5.15 have no reset-progress shortcut; use Clear storage/Clear data below.
  • Clear storage/Clear data for Leafbound in Android's app settings removes the app's local progress, preferences and recordings. Uninstalling normally removes app-specific data too.

Leafbound has no cloud restoration service. It is configured to exclude its data from Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup. Device or system behavior can vary. Bytes Alchemist does not receive the game's local files and cannot recover them or remotely delete copies on your device.

Security and device permissions

Leafbound relies on Android's app-specific storage and device security controls. It does not add its own encryption to saved sound and progress files, and no device or storage method can be guaranteed secure. Keep your device protected and current.

The Android version declares microphone permission for optional recordings, plus audio-settings and legacy Bluetooth permissions used by its audio support. It has no Internet permission and does not request location, contacts, camera or shared-storage permissions.

Support messages and platform services

If you choose to email bytes.alchemist@gmail.com, Bytes Alchemist receives your email address and whatever message or attachments you send. This is separate from the offline game. The mailbox is hosted by Gmail, so Google processes those messages to provide email services. We use support messages to respond and resolve requests, retain them only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal requirements, and do not use them for advertising. You can request deletion by email, subject to any necessary legal retention. Do not send passwords or sensitive recordings.

Google Play and Android provide distribution, device and platform services under Google's own privacy practices. This policy does not replace the Google Privacy Policy.

Questions from parents or guardians

Leafbound does not ask players to enter their name, age or contact details, and it does not create online player profiles. The local-storage and optional-recording practices above apply to every player. Parents or guardians can disable microphone permission and use the deletion controls described above. Contact Bytes Alchemist with privacy questions or concerns. This section does not declare a target age group or content rating.
